# Lumora Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 1 November 2026_

Lumora (CodeAlpha · by Rajesh Ranjan) is a student project built during the CodeAlpha internship. This page explains what data the app handles.

## What we collect
- **Account details:** name, username, email address and a hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).
- **Content you post:** photos, videos, reels, stories, captions, comments and the music track you attach.
- **Messages:** chats are end-to-end encrypted in your browser. The server stores only encrypted text and encrypted media, and cannot read them.
- **Device notifications (optional):** if you turn them on, your browser's push subscription is stored so we can send alerts. You can turn them off at any time.

## How we use it
- To run the app: sign-in, your feed, follows, notifications and chat.
- To send you a one-time verification or password-reset code by email.

## Gmail
Lumora sends verification emails from its own Gmail account using Google's OAuth. The app does **not** access, read or store anyone's Gmail inbox or any Google user data.

## Where data is stored
- Account and post data: MongoDB Atlas.
- Photos and videos: Cloudinary.
- Hosting: Render.

## Sharing
We do not sell your data and do not share it with advertisers. Data is shared only with the services above that are needed to run the app.

## Your choices
You can delete your own posts and messages in the app. To delete your account or ask any question about your data, email **lumoracodealpha.in@gmail.com**.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
